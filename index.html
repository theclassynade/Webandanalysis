Analysing vulnerability statistics for intrusion tolerance

How differently do Apache, Nginx, and IIS actually break?

This dashboard summarises the dissertation's empirical NVD analysis of three web-server platforms — publication volume and severity, weakness-type (CWE) profile, patch-timeliness proxies, and pairwise CWE overlap, the basis for judging whether platform diversity is a viable intrusion-tolerance strategy at the web-server tier.

4.5 · Annual publication trend

Apache's CVE count is consistently populated across every study year; Nginx and IIS's counts cluster in a small number of years, with several years showing zero recorded CVEs — plausibly real quiet periods, but also consistent with a smaller, less continuously tracked CPE population.
